Care notes worth keeping

The questions we're asked at the counter every single week.

Pothos, ZZ plant, and snake plant are the three we'd bet on in a north-facing room. Leaf growth slows in winter — that's the plant conserving energy, not dying, so ease off watering rather than adding more light.

Push a finger two knuckles into the soil. Dry at that depth, water thoroughly until it runs from the drainage hole. Wet still, wait a few days and check again. Calendars water plants on your schedule, not theirs.

Usually a light problem before it's a feeding problem. Move it a metre closer to the window before you reach for fertiliser — most flats have more usable light a little further from the wall than people expect.

Everything tagged "pet-safe" in the collection above is non-toxic if chewed, though we'd still keep the trailing ones out of easy reach — curiosity does the rest, regardless of toxicity.
